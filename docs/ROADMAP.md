# Roadmap

Ordered by what makes Sprint better for someone using it tomorrow.

## Next
1. **Accounts and sync** (email, Apple, Google) on top of the existing snapshot + event log. Local use stays first.
2. **Calendar awareness** via `CalendarProvider`: "You have 35 minutes before your next meeting. Run a 25-minute sprint?"
3. **Spoken coach** with ElevenLabs through `/api/tts`: encouragement at start and completion, opt-in.
4. **Mission artwork** through `ImageProvider`: a real generator behind the same palette fallback.
5. **Learning**: preferred focus times and typical overrun per sprint type, on device, feeding the planner's sizing.

## Later
- Focus music and ambient sound (`MusicProvider`).
- Apple Watch and Wear OS: the dial, pause and complete.
- Shared missions and accountability for families and small teams.
- A service worker for fully offline reloads on the web.
- Subscription: AI planning, adaptive planning, voice coach, visuals, advanced analytics, unlimited missions. Never in the first-run experience.

## Not planned
A chat screen as the primary interface. Streaks. Leaderboards.
